oEmbed unfurler control

Same oEmbed shape as /oembed-poc/, but the dimensions are quoted numbers ("1200", "630", "300", "200") instead of a lone quote.

This one is expected to unfurl on both the pre-fix and post-fix build (only the lone quote panics). Its job is to prove the oEmbed document is actually requested and parsed, so a quiet result on the lone-quote page cannot be mistaken for "the extractor never ran".
